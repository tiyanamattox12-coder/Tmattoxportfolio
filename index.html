About

I'm a sophomore at Harvard studying Mechanical Engineering on the Mechanical Systems track, with a secondary in Afro-Latino & Caribbean Studies, expecting to graduate in May 2028. My coursework spans mechanics, MATLAB-based signal analysis, and computational thinking in Python.

Outside the lab, I'm the social chair for the Harvard Society of Black Scientists & Engineers, a member of Harvard's varsity cheerleading team, and a regular volunteer with youth programs in Cambridge.
